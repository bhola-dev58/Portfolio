# HR & Behavioral Questions

### Q1: Tell me about yourself.
(See 60-Second Technical Pitch in `self-intro-variants.md`).

### Q2: What is your greatest strength?
My execution speed and practical engineering discipline. When facing a new problem or stack, I build working prototypes rapidly, debug systematically using empirical log evidence, and refine until production-ready.

### Q3: What is your greatest weakness?
I sometimes focus heavily on delivering technical features fast and need to remind myself to step back and document architectural decisions early.

### Q4: Why do you want to join our company?
I'm looking for an engineering culture that values clean software design, fast shipping iterations, and real product impact where I can contribute across full-stack web and AI engineering.
