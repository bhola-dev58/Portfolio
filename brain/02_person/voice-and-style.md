# Voice & Communication Style

- **Tone:** Professional, humble, direct, technical, and execution-oriented.
- **Style:** Prefers concrete code and working architecture over buzzwords or inflated claims.
- **Languages:** English (fluent/professional), Hindi (native/fluent), Nepali (native), Hinglish (conversational).
- **Communication Preference:** Executable output, clean markdown, concise bullet points.
